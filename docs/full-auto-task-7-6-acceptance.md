## Retained live protocol evidence (Task 7.4)

The recorded exercise completed 20 takes with 20 linked outputs and unique take, shot, and attempt identities. All five saved stages were represented with counts 5/5/5/4/1. The run used 20 assistant reservations within the authorized 40-call limit and required no image rerolls. All 20 PNGs were valid at 832 × 1216.

A controlled backend stop during an acknowledged attempt was followed by supported cancellation and an unchanged-scope Resume after restart. Resume adopted the already completed first output and completed the other 19 targets under the original run. After the ComfyUI restart, the first remote history entry was unavailable; its retained PNG marker and bytes matched the pre-restart copy. The remaining 19 outputs matched current history by attempt and generation metadata. Task 7.4 records two recovery fixes: separator normalization for Windows output subfolders and comparison against the original selection receipt when server normalization changes the displayed full-body intent. Regression cases are named in `tests/test_full_auto_correlation_independent.py`, `tests/test_full_auto_startup_independent.py`, and `tests/test_full_auto_conflict_recovery_journeys.py`.

This is direct protocol evidence for one authorized run. It does not certify all recovery states or make local FakeComfy tests equivalent to the live exercise.

## Retained visual evidence (Task 7.5)

All 20 outputs were opened and compared with their saved stage, coverage, accessory state, and framing. The progressive accessory appeared in the 19 outputs at stages 0–3 and was absent from the stage 4 output. The final prepared and queued positive prompts had no positive accessory mention, and the permanent appearance projection omitted it.

The review found a garment-fidelity mismatch in take 7 even though stored wardrobe wording appeared verbatim in prepared and queued prompts. At least one foot was visible in five outputs; the other 15 cropped the feet out. None showed the subject head-to-toe with both feet visible, despite all 20 retaining a positive framing instruction. One prepared positive contained the exact canonical framing sentence; each of the 20 take-specific framing lines appeared verbatim in both prepared and queued positives. No explicit close/cropped-shot instruction appeared in the final positives.

This evidence records a pixel-level framing mismatch and one garment mismatch. Visibility of other body regions is partial or occluded, so coverage is unverified where pixels do not show the region. Successful output and prompt consistency do not establish visual fidelity.

## Retained manual journey

The manual path remains explicit end to end: create or select a saved look; edit and publish a version-bound profile in Looks; optionally apply that exact look/profile to a session and preview/apply wardrobe progression; prepare takes; load and approve current review; submit selected prepared takes; then explicitly click Run. Opening the progression panel has no write side effect. Navigating from review does not submit or run, and approval remains a separate action. The UI tests `SessionView.fullAuto.test.jsx` cases `requires explicit review approval and navigation does not submit or run` and `retains the manual prepare, approve, submit, and Run journey after settled transfer` exercise these boundaries. `SessionLookProgression.test.jsx` covers explicit look/profile/progression operations; `tests/test_task10_4_manual_journey.py::test_offline_manual_journey_and_automatic_draft_stop_at_review` covers the manual API/service journey through preparation and review; `Resources.test.jsx`, `Looks.test.jsx`, and `AutomationProfileEditor.test.jsx` cover creation and reusable-look setup.
